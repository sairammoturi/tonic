Connect your Casio with a USB cable so Tonic can hear every key you press.

How to connect your Casio

  1. Find the USB port on the back of the keyboard. It may be a square USB-B port marked "USB" or "to Host", or a micro-USB / USB-C port on newer Casiotone models.
  2. Plug it into your phone. Use a USB-B to USB-C cable, or your keyboard's normal USB cable with a USB-C OTG adapter.
  3. Switch the keyboard on, tap Connect keyboard above and allow access to MIDI devices.
  4. Press any key. Its name should appear above.

This works in Chrome on Android. Older models with only round 5-pin MIDI sockets need a USB-MIDI adapter. In the Claude chat preview, USB devices may be blocked, so use the full app link to connect.
